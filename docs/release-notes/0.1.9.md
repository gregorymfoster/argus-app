- **Search your Library.** Press ⌘K and type words or a description ("sunset on the water", "Lisbon"), and the matches fill the grid. Search by meaning downloads its model once, the first time you use it. Save a search as an Album in the sidebar.
- **Keywords and captions.** Every Photo gets a caption and suggested Keywords, with Places nested as `Places|Portugal|Lisbon`. Accept them all in the Loupe or add your own with K. They're written to XMP on export.
- **Your own Presets.** Create Preset (⇧⌘N) saves the settings you pick from a Photo into a Yours group, next to the 57 built-in ones.
- **More precise Masks.** Each Mask has an Amount, components that Add, Subtract or Intersect, and Luminance and Color range masks.
- **Straighten.** Draw along a horizon or wall in Crop (or ⌘-drag) and the photo levels itself, as in Lightroom.
- **HEIC export and output sharpening.** Export can write HEIC on the Mac, size by short edge or megapixels, and sharpen for screens.
- **Auto edits closer to yours.** Auto now learns from your own exports and looks at the kind of scene first. Greg preferred it to the old Auto 9 times out of 11.
- **A sharper cull.** The rules check catches camera shake, leaves portraits alone, and learns from your flags as you cull. A Stars-sorted grid keeps its order while you rate.
- **Smaller fixes.** ⌘/ shows every shortcut, J cycles tile styles, AI takes sit in Edit's panel, and the cull ends with a next step. Holding ⌘Z can no longer freeze the app, and Library writes wait their turn instead of failing.
