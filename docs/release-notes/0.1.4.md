- **A calmer AI cull.** Argus now gives every photo a star rating and only rejects frames with a clear flaw, like missed focus or heavy blur. It never picks for you, so the real cull stays yours: sort by stars and go.
- **Bursts become Stacks.** Runs of near-identical frames are grouped automatically. Press N to look at a Stack side by side and Enter to keep the one you like.
- **Suggestions learn your style.** The edits Argus offers start to lean toward how you finish the takes you keep.
- **Sort the grid** by capture time, stars, flag or file name (⇧S jumps to stars).
- **Heavy AI work waits its turn.** Culling a whole card waits until your Mac is idle and plugged in, or overnight. Click Run now to skip the wait. A small meter under Background work shows what the AI is using.
- **A lighter, quieter app.** The grid and background work use much less CPU when nothing is happening, and every screen has a cleaner, denser look.
