- **Cull, Edit and Export modes.** Every screen has a mode switch at the top: Library (⌘1), Cull (⌘2), Edit (⌘3) and Export (⌘4), each showing how far that pass has got.
- **Cull moment by moment.** Cull walks every photo one moment at a time (a Stack, or a short run of singles). ⇧↓ shows the next moment too, and ⇧M merges two Stacks. Each choice you make here teaches the AI cull.
- **Edit your Picks in one list.** Edit mode lists your Picks by where they stand: match ready, not edited, or edited.
- **Export as its own step.** Export mode gathers the Export settings in one place.
