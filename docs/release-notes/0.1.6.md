- **AI Denoise.** Clean up high-ISO noise with a model that runs on your Mac, at the top of the Detail panel. A progress bar shows how long it has left, and the Amount slider sets how much of the noise it removes.
- **Copy, paste and sync settings,** as in Lightroom. Pick which groups to paste (Basic, Detail, spots and more), or sync one photo's settings across a selection.
- **Heal and Clone.** Remove dust spots and small distractions by copying pixels from elsewhere in the same photo. Nothing is generated. Press / to try a different source area.
- **Lens corrections for Sony cameras.** Argus reads the distortion, vignetting and color fringing corrections your camera already stores in each RAW and applies them for you.
- **Presets.** A built-in library of 57 presets, sorted into Lightroom's familiar groups, each with an Amount slider. Argus suggests which ones suit each Shoot.
- **A brighter third suggestion.** The three AI edits are now Natural, Punchy and Bright.
- **Fixes.** A damaged RAW file no longer crashes Argus. Flags always land on the right photo, Esc closes a menu without clearing your selection, and quick Undo and Redo stay in order. Importing the same card twice copies into the matching Shoot folder.
