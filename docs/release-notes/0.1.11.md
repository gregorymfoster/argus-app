- **A new look.** Argus has a new glass camera icon, a welcome banner on first launch, and new art for empty and finished screens, like an empty Library or a Shoot with nothing left to cull.
- **Motion that answers you.** Flags stamp on, counts tick, the key you pressed lights up in the bar, and a photo grows from its grid tile into the Loupe or Edit. It only plays when you cause a change, and Reduce motion turns all of it off.
- **One card, several Shoots.** When a card holds more than one outing, Import proposes a Shoot for each, names them from their photos, and lets you remove a split or drag photos between groups. In the grid, right-click to Move to Shoot or Split Shoot here.
- **Edit the whole Shoot at once.** In Edit, press W to see the rest of the Shoot under the photo you're editing, matched to each frame's light as you drag. Lock look (⇧L) keeps the Shoot following your later edits.
- **Crop like this.** Crop one photo by hand and frames of the same shape shot within two minutes of it are offered the same crop, with Accept all in the grid.
- **Smarter style matching.** Apply to the rest now reads all of a Shoot's hand edits together and falls back on your overall style for frames unlike any you edited. There's an off switch in Settings.
- **Better burst picks.** In a burst, frames that don't cut off anyone's head now lead.
