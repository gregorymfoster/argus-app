- **A much faster AI cull.** The check for broken frames (missed focus, heavy blur or shake) now uses rules instead of the local model, and it runs right after Import. A whole card takes seconds rather than most of an hour, and it still never rejects a good frame on our test cards. You can still ask the model for a second opinion.
- **Better burst order.** Inside a Stack, frames where everyone's head is upright come first. On a test card, that picked the frame Greg kept in about three bursts out of four.
- **Clearer Stacks in the grid.** A collapsed Stack looks like a small pile with a count, and an expanded one sits on its own tinted band with each frame numbered. Press S on any frame to open or close it, or right-click to expand or collapse every Stack at once.
- **Progress you can cancel.** Auto all and the AI cull share one compact progress row with a time estimate and a Cancel button. Auto all now shows its progress in the chat and under Background work, and Undo reverts the whole batch.
