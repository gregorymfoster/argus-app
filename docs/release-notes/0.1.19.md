- **Work mode (⌘5).** One screen shows how far a new Shoot has got: when it will be ready, each stage, where the time goes, and what the Mac's CPU, GPU and memory are doing.
- **Run it all (⇧⌘R).** A new Shoot's heavy work (Suggestions, denoised bases for likely keepers) runs up front at full speed, skipping the idle wait and keeping the Mac awake. Cull drops it back to your Compute level. Show in Finder moves to ⌘R, as in Lightroom Classic.
- **Import and prepare.** A card import with Suggest edits starts preparing by itself after 20 seconds unless you touch it, then lands you in Work mode.
- **Finish.** Export gets a Finish step that adds Keywords and runs your backups.
- **Backups to other places.** Keep copies of the Library on another disk or in your Google Drive or iCloud folder. Each copy opens as a Library, only new files are copied, nothing is ever deleted, and copies are checked once a month.
- **Bigger, smarter exports.** An 8K long edge and any custom size (never upscaled), a Remove location switch that really strips GPS, and an Estimate of file sizes, total, time and free space before you export.
- **Display P3 and watermarks.** Export in Display P3 to keep wide colours, and add a text watermark in any corner.
- **Add to Apple Photos.** Exported Photos can go straight into Photos, in an album named after the Shoot. macOS asks once for permission.
- **Recommended preset.** JPEG 85 at full size, measured on A7R V files. Export with Previous (⌥⇧⌘E) repeats your last export, and the Name field no longer shrinks to a sliver.
