- **Cull Rounds.** After a first pass, run another Cull over just your Picks, regrouped into moments, to narrow them down.
- **Crop and straighten.** The AI straightens crooked horizons and walls across a Shoot (and can tighten in on loosely framed people if you ask). Review it all in one Edit popover (⌥⌘R) and apply it as a single Undo.
- **Auto Denoise.** The AI sets each photo's AI Denoise amount from how noisy its RAW actually is.
- **Apply look carries Quick fixes.** Applying a look to a Shoot now also brings AI Masks, face exposure, detail and noise to every frame.
- **One Status bar.** Every mode shares a Status bar along the bottom of the window with the AI meter, running work and app updates.
- **Flags stick.** Pressing Pick on a Pick, or Reject on a Reject, keeps it. Only U clears a flag.
- **Clear flags at a glance.** Picks, Rejects and photos still needing a flag now look clearly different.
- **Big Stacks in pages.** A large Stack comes in pages sized to your screen so you can compare the frames, then a final look at its Picks.
- **Apply look to Shoot.** One popover replaces Apply to rest, the Shoot look strip and Lock look, and hovering an AI take previews it on the photo.
- **Arrow keys in Edit.** ← and → move through the photos, and dragging a slider no longer keeps the keys.
- **A steadier Cull.** Portraits in a big grid render sharp, not as small tiles. Cull mode comes back to moments you skipped, N opens Cull mode, and the cull progress bar no longer jumps backwards.
- **Keys stay where you type.** Keys pressed in a dialog no longer reach the screen behind it, Esc that closes a dropdown keeps you in Export, and only a quick tap of Space zooms.
- **Cleaner previews.** Live renders never flash the previous photo's result, frames keep their shape while re-rendering, and the Navigator box sits right on portraits.
- **Sturdier AI pass.** No duplicate takes jobs, a rejected first frame no longer costs its burst its takes, MLX builds are found for any model, and one bad reply skips one photo instead of spoiling the batch.
- **Fewer hangs.** A damaged RAW header can't hang previews, a stray backup name can't crash a restore, and a failed Import doesn't block the next one.
